# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want Agent Protocols to be a productive and respectful open-source specification community. Participants are expected to communicate with care, assume good intent, and focus criticism on ideas and protocol behavior rather than people.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Explain tradeoffs and assumptions clearly.
- Welcome implementation feedback from different languages, runtimes, and deployment models.
- Keep discussions focused on interoperability, safety, and practical adoption.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of specification discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or restrict participation when behavior harms the community or blocks productive work.
